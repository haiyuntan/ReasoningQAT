- Binning fixed to empirical quintile boundaries, ties preserved and duplicate boundaries collapsed; no outcome-optimized bins, scatter exploration or alternative favorable layouts were tried. Flat or negative curves remain.
- P0-only generation is unavailable. P0/P4 paired plotting is explicitly on P4-origin candidates; length curves intentionally overlap.
- V0 and V3 selected by seed2 uniform choice from sorted confirmation valid/invalid candidates. V1/V2 are structurally unsupported, not evidence of zero discordance between independently generated models. No new generation was added for prettier cases.
- Main heatmap uses two real tactics × two models with one common nats scale. Full text and token-ID source records remain available. Individual BPE bytes that cannot decode as UTF-8 show #token_id on ticks instead of replacement glyphs; no token IDs or statistics are changed.
- Fig3 displays the prespecified primary result unchanged; effective mixed-label theorem support and low-power status retained.
- Violin/box distribution and theorem mean dots convey repeated candidates without implying token/candidate independence. Optional incomplete model pools omitted and recorded in the data snapshot.
- No empirical results, threshold, lens, dataset split or primary endpoint was changed. Validation found isolated byte-BPE fragments decoding to replacement glyphs. Tick labels now use #token_id for these fragments; full tactic text is preserved in A2 and exact token IDs in CSV/JSONL. No values or case choices changed.
